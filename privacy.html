WaveDrop – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

WaveDrop respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the WaveDrop desktop application.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, WaveDrop may process:

  • Video links and URLs submitted by users.
  • Download preferences and application settings.
  • Account information provided through supported third-party login services.
  • Basic profile information made available through authorized account connections.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide and improve WaveDrop features, including account connection, video previews, download management, and application functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

WaveDrop may interact with third-party platforms. When you connect an account, those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies. WaveDrop does not claim ownership of third-party content or user accounts.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, or protect the application and its users.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users can remove locally stored application data by deleting the relevant application data and settings. Information handled by third-party services may need to be managed through those services directly.

8. Children's Privacy

WaveDrop is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
